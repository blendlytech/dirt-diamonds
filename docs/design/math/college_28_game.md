## 2. Recovery, eligibility and ace usage

**Settled (owner, 2026-10-18):** `MinRestDays(College) = 3`, implemented in 13-2; re-prove the 28-game cadence. A day count is the calendar-date difference from the last start: Wed→Sat = 3, Sat→Wed = 4. It is **not** the number of fully idle intervening days (2 and 3). Persist dates across weeks, year rollover and reload; a never-started available pitcher is rested. Current `PitcherFatigue` resets pitches at construction and contains no multi-day recovery reservoir. Proposed recovery contract is a threshold on start dates plus the short-rest capacity modifier below, not an invented daily restoration percentage. Relief appearances must not silently become starts; any separate relief-workload recovery policy needs its own approved contract.

Keep §6's deterministic selection: filter absences, then series-used starters, then take the highest-rated starter with date gap ≥3 under the approved College value. If none meets rest, take the best-rested available, unused starter. Only when that set is empty may a reliever open. Never bypass the series-used filter on fallback; a legal pitcher/lineup must exist or the game forfeits. **13-2 implemented contract, shared across HS, College and Pro:** one starter-quality scalar, mean effective stuff/control before in-game fatigue; ties use stamina descending, then player ID ascending. Fallback sorts date-gap descending first, then that same ordering. 13-2 owns this single definition and its cross-tier tests; College must not introduce a private scorer. The owner approved this scalar in 13-2 D2; preserve its refresh/re-Initialize parity. No new random draw or probabilistic ace weighting.

| Week 1 Saturday | A | 3 days, eligible |

### 2.1 Capacity and injury proposals

Current `PitcherFatigue.cs:17–25,45–84` gives capacity `C = 70 + 0.5 × effectiveStamina`, comfort through 0.6C, multiplier `max(0.5, 1 − 0.25 × ((p/C − 0.6)/0.4)^2)` thereafter. PED-adjusted stamina uses integer half-up ×1.5 clamped to 100; healthy all-50 capacity is 95. There are no `ShortRestCapacityPenalty` / `OveruseInjuryRisk` constants implemented in this class. The spec names them without values.

**Candidate Math values, not settled:** College `ShortRestCapacityPenalty = 0.15` (fraction), `OveruseInjuryRisk = 0.01` (additional independent probability per short-rest start). For gap<3 only, use `Cshort = C × (1 − 0.15)`; for gap≥3 exactly C. At stamina 50 without PED, this is 80.75 versus 95 pitches. No stack per missing rest day and no penalty on fully rested starts. Apply at game initialization once, after effective stamina; do not reduce the rating and capacity again in a second engine. Macro remains complete-game without in-game fatigue as §6 requires, but uses the same selection and injury exposure. These are initial candidates to test, not a requirement to make a weakened pitcher win less by editing the resolver.

If ordinary start injury probability is q, an additional independent 1% hazard gives total `1 − (1−q)(1−.01)` on short rest; never add unclamped probabilities or emit duplicate absences. Route through the shipped absence/event owner, preserving transaction and Life/Baseball boundaries. Do not invent a cross-loop write. No extra RNG draw for a fully rested start or disabled/neutral feature; document the College-only draw order. For k short-rest starts the isolated extra-hazard chance is `1−.99^k`: k=1 →1%, k=14 →13.13%, k=28 →24.53%. These are conditional analytic exposure numbers, not observed season injury rates (injury changes later exposure).

Compare candidates penalty {0.10,0.15,0.20}, risk {.005,.01,.02} on the fixed training seeds, freeze one before holdout. Proposed acceptance: healthy fixture has zero added hazard/penalty; all short-rest starts meet fallback legality; penalty lowers capacity by exactly its fraction; risk empirical frequency over 100,000 isolated forced short-rest starts lies within ±0.001 of the chosen probability. Pair season runs with/without risk using separate, recorded injury streams to distinguish outcome noise; report starts, absences and days lost, not just wins. No target season injury prevalence is invented before duration/ordinary hazard are validated with the implementation owner.

**R1-MATH dependency:** current `PitcherFatigue.EffectiveRatings` at lines 94 onward contracts stuff/control toward 50, which improves a sub-50 pitcher and leaves all-50 unchanged. Lowering capacity alone amplifies that defect. The accepted fatigue contract forbids improvement. 13-2/13-3 may prove date selection independently, but acceptance of attended short-rest effects requires the reviewed R1-MATH correction and effective-rating ordering. Sweep ratings 0,30,50,85,100, PED off/on, pitches 0/comfort/capacity/over-capacity; increasing workload must never improve effective pitching ability and fully rested/disabled paths stay exact. This plan does not authorize rewriting the fatigue curve, fixing Pro, or rebaselining guards.

## 3. National Ranking and stability

Current *spec*, not built `RankingCalculator`: `0.50 W + 0.30 SOS + 0.20 RD_norm`. **Proposal: retain 0.50/0.30/0.20** pending evidence; doubled games do not justify an automatic weight change. Proposed exact definitions for 13-4: W=wins/games; SOS = sum of each scheduled opponent's current wins divided by sum of their current games, counted once per completed head-to-head meeting (repeat opponents weighted by exposure); `RD_norm = (clamp((RF−RA)/G,−5,5)+5)/10`. Use all completed regular-season results, including forfeit finals, exclude postseason. Zero games → W=.5 and RD_norm=.5; SOS=.5 when its denominator is zero. This interprets 'opponents combined win%' literally; do not switch to opponent-excluded records without labelling a formula revision. Proposed exact-score ties: W descending, RD/game descending, team ID ascending; record deterministic ID bias in equal-team fixtures. Freeze selection rank for series hosting; weekly display updates cannot retroactively change seeds.

**Identifiability finding:** no regular-season cross-conference games exist. W, SOS and RD compare teams *within* two disconnected graphs. Twice as many games reduces sampling noise but cannot identify which entire conference is stronger. A stronger conference with equal internal teams can have the same expected records as a weaker one. No choice of these three weights resolves this. This is a reportable limitation of settled schedule input, not authorization to add games, hidden ratings, or conference quotas. Seeds 1–17 are a stable rule-based ranking, not a proven global-strength ordering.

## 4. The Red Dirt Series

Input is fixed: Thu/Fri/Sat after conference finals, better selection rank hosts games 1/3, first to two wins, no Sunday, no repeat starter anywhere in the same series. A starter from the previous Saturday has a 5-day gap on Thursday and is eligible at approved min 3. With four healthy SP and the usual A > B > C > D ordering:

| Game | Starter | Reason |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Thursday 1 | A | Rested, best eligible; conference-final start was 5 days ago |
| Friday 2 | B | A excluded by series membership even if a rest override exists |
| Saturday 3, only at 1–1 | C | A/B excluded; C rested; D remains available |

Analytic bullpen-opening rate for this fixture is **0%**, including game 3; not an estimate for injured rosters. With exactly two unused-eligible SP at series entry and no returns, game 3 requires an RP if played; with one SP, games 2/3 require relief opening; no eligible pitcher means forfeit. Injuries/returns during the series require game-day reevaluation. A returning unused starter may qualify; a returning already-used starter never does. Track opener identity across reload, including an RP promoted/opening, so the no-repeat rule cannot be bypassed by role changes. Any ambiguity about repeat RP openers should be resolved conservatively by recording every game's starter ID, independent of roster role, for Opus confirmation.

For independent game wins by team A with probabilities p1,p2, `P(game 3) = p1(1−p2)+(1−p1)p2`. At equal strengths p1=p2=.5 →50%; at constant p=.7 →42%. Expected final length is 2+P(game 3), i.e. 2.5 for equal independent games. If venue reverses a .55 advantage (.55,.45), the independent number is .505. Correlated outcomes, different starters and injury dynamics invalidate a naive common-p formula; report simulated conditional rates. These are sanity calculations, never a target for competitive series or a reason to force a deciding game.

**Proof / pass:** proposed `RunCollegeSeriesSuite` in the MC command; exhaustive two-win paths AA, BB, ABA, ABB, BAA, BAB must terminate in 2/3 games, emit one champion and never credit an unplayed game. Verify host order, dates, once-per-series starts, fallback, absence/return and reload cases. For a standalone fair independent coin fixture, 100,000 series must yield deciding-game fraction .495–.505 (proposed statistical check). For actual generated champions, log deciding-game count and game-3 relief openers / played game-3 team-starts, with binomial Wilson 95% intervals; also log per-series probability of either team needing relief. Do not count unplayed game 3 as a rested starter success. No actual bullpen-frequency target is defensible before availability is implemented.
